Privacy PolicyBrewdex · Effective 24 October 2026

In short: Brewdex does not collect, sell or share any personal data. What you enter is stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We can't see it.

Information you enter

The coffees, recipes, tasting results, experiments and taster names you add are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, Brewdex also syncs them to your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so they are restored when you reinstall the app or use another iPhone with the same Apple ID. This data belongs to your Apple Account and is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it, and it is never sent to us or to any other third party.

Data we collect

None. Brewdex has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server operated by us.

Third parties

Brewdex does not include third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking. Apple stores your synced data in iCloud as described above, and may include app data in iCloud backups of your iPhone.

Turning off sync

To keep your data on your iPhone only, turn off iCloud for Brewdex in Settings → your name → iCloud. You can turn it back on at any time.

Deleting your data

Deleting Brewdex removes its data from your iPhone. If you use iCloud sync, a copy stays in your iCloud account so it can be restored. To remove that copy too, delete Brewdex's data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

Brewdex does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
