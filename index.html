BrewdexCoffee Rankings for iPhone

Brewdex turns coffee tasting into a ranked league. Taste two coffees or recipes side by side, pick a winner for each flavor, and Brewdex builds your personal leaderboard using Elo ratings.

Get help

Found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest a feature? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

How do the ratings work?
Every coffee and recipe starts at the same rating. Each time you pick a winner in a head-to-head tasting, the winner gains points and the loser loses some, more so when the result is a surprise. The OVR score on each player card is that rating shown on a 10–99 scale.

Can I just give scores instead?
Yes. Brewdex starts in Simple mode, where you rate each characteristic from 1 to 5. Switch to Elo mode in Settings for head-to-head rankings.

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud account. Brewdex has no account of its own and no servers, and we can't see your data.

What happens if I delete the app?
With iCloud on, your coffees, recipes and rankings come back when you reinstall Brewdex. Without iCloud, deleting the app deletes your data.

Does Brewdex sync between devices?
Yes, between iPhones signed in to the same Apple ID. Requires Brewdex 1.2 or later.
